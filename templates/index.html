About Dorm Expense Tracker

This workspace keeps each dorm admin’s data separate and splits common costs fairly among residents. Use this guide to understand the roles, tabs, and bill calculation rules.

User Roles & Access

  • Admin – registers the dorm, manages residents, expenses, fixed utilities, billing periods, and can delete data.
  • Renter – logs in from the admin’s invite link; can review totals, upload receipts, and adjust their own abono credits but cannot change shared settings.

Admins can invite renters by copying the link in the header; renters enter that invite token during “Create Renter Account”.

Tabs Overview

Residents
Track each resident’s days present, per-utility share, rent portion, abono credits, and final balance. Expand a row to see the detailed formula used.
Time Away
Admins record when a resident stayed out. Days away reduce that resident’s variable share for water, electric, and internet.
Abono
Admins and renters can enter general abono (shared purchases) and mineral abono credits and upload supporting receipts.
Fixed
Admins define fixed-cost appliances or services (e.g., internet modem, refrigerator) that every tenant splits equally before daily usage is calculated.
Checking
Shows whether totals balance, compares resident dues vs. grand total, and highlights mismatch messages so you can correct entries.
Admin
Admins set the billing period (defaults to 15th of last month – 14th of current) and input monthly expenses for water, electric, internet, and rent.

Billing Logic

  • Fixed charges are split equally among all residents.
  • Remaining (variable) utility amounts are prorated by the number of days a resident was present in the billing period.
  • Rent is divided evenly across residents.
  • Abono credits (general + mineral) are subtracted from each resident’s gross total.
  • The Checking tab confirms totals minus credits equal what residents owe.

Workflow Tips

  1. Register or log in as admin, invite renters via the header button.
  2. Add residents, record their away dates, and enter utilities, rent, fixed costs, and abono credits.
  3. Have renters log in, upload receipts, and confirm their balances.
  4. Use the Checking tab to ensure the period balances before collecting payments.
